ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC · BUSINESS SERVICES

Advertising and creator collaboration services

ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC helps businesses explore advertising and creator collaborations. We currently accept inquiries for review; this website does not offer instant campaign purchase, live creator inventory booking, or a public payment checkout.

What you can inquire about

  • Advertising planning: discuss campaign objectives, audiences, markets, channels and the kind of creator participation you seek.
  • Creator collaboration evaluation: review the intended creator format, audience fit and proposed campaign requirements. Any specific creator participation remains subject to verification, agreement and availability.
  • Creative brief coordination: organize proposed messaging, creative direction, concept requirements and a review plan for a potential separately approved engagement.

Campaign Planning & Coordination — planning-only service

Businesses may request an individually scoped proposal for a planning-only service consisting of five proposed deliverables:

  1. Campaign discovery brief: goals, audience, market and platform requirements.
  2. Creator/channel suitability plan: criteria and suitable formats, with no creator booked or guaranteed.
  3. Creative direction outline: message approach, concepts, brand safety and review requirements; finished ad media is not included.
  4. Coordination schedule: milestones, client responsibilities and review gates, subject to the accepted order.
  5. Final planning summary: recommendations, documented decisions and suggested next steps.

Each proposal would identify its actual deliverable formats, revision count, timing, quoted price and currency, taxes, costs, intellectual-property rights, customer terms and approval responsibilities. Submitting an inquiry is free and creates no order or payment obligation. No creator placement, live ad publication, impressions or sales are guaranteed.

Request a planning-service proposal using the existing advertiser inquiry form.

How a commercial engagement would work

  1. Inquiry: submit the advertiser form and describe the services sought.
  2. Scope and quotation: ALPHA would review availability and, if appropriate, present a written proposal identifying the provider, service deliverables, applicable market, quoted amount and transaction currency, taxes/fees, rights, schedule, revision/approval steps and service limitations.
  3. Written approval: a commercial order would require separate acceptance of the proposed scope, price, fulfillment conditions and applicable cancellation/refund terms before any payment.
  4. Delivery and review: any agreed service would follow its signed order and confirmed milestones; no delivery date, placement or result is promised by submitting an inquiry.

Pricing and payment availability

There is no universal fixed-price package advertised here. Any prospective work is individually scoped and quoted in a stated currency before acceptance. No online checkout is enabled on this public site. ALPHA does not request card details or collect payment through these inquiry forms.

Service limitations

Submission does not create a customer account, reserve a creator, authorize paid outreach or purchase advertising. Creator dashboards, payment tools and campaign execution are not available for general public use. There is no guarantee of impressions, sales, conversions, creator earnings or campaign results.

Before any paid order

ALPHA must provide the applicable commercial agreement, service delivery expectations, customer support channel, and cancellation/refund/dispute procedures. The existing Public Interest Terms and Public Interest Privacy Notice concern inquiries; they are not a substitute for an agreed paid service contract.
