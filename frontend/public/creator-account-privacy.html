Creator Account Privacy Notice

Effective November 22, 2026. Notice version creator-privacy-2026-11-22-v2. ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC (“ALPHA”) is responsible for this creator-account application process when creator registration is enabled. Publishing this notice does not itself open registration.

Information, purposes and receipt

Supabase Auth receives the email address, password and confirmation/security information directly over HTTPS. The ALPHA business application does not receive the plaintext password. The creator application receives the verified issuer identity reference and creator/channel display name, two-letter country code, primary HTTPS channel URL and optional primary language. It records the opaque subject, creator ID, request key and digest, explicit processing and terms choices, the separate optional marketing choice, version identifiers, timestamps, and a PENDING_REVIEW registration receipt. Infrastructure security logs may record limited technical access information.

These details are needed to verify the sign-in, receive an application, limit duplicate or fraudulent submissions, carry out manual channel/eligibility review, secure tenant access, and document what the applicant accepted. A submitted URL does not establish ownership of a channel, rights to content or eligibility for advertising. Applicants remain UNVERIFIED and do not automatically receive private creator-directory, marketplace, campaign or payment access.

Providers, locations and separate inquiries

Supabase supplies authentication; Railway hosts the application API and PostgreSQL records. Vercel may serve public information pages. Processing or security records may be handled in different countries depending on provider operation and applicable requirements. A public creator interest form and any optional HighLevel CRM follow-up are separate from this authenticated creator registration. Submitting an account application alone is not a marketing opt-in or authorization to contact others.

Choice and withdrawal

Submission requires acknowledgement of account-processing information and acceptance of the creator account registration terms. Optional marketing is separate and initially unchecked. We record the submitted choices in an immutable receipt; a historical receipt does not establish perpetual permission after a valid withdrawal. Requests to withdraw optional marketing or exercise applicable privacy rights require human handling.

Retention, deletion and rights

Application, review, security and receipt records are retained as needed for review, an active account, dispute handling, prevention of abuse or legal obligations. Following closure, rejection or an eligible deletion request, ALPHA reviews which records can be erased or restricted while preserving legally necessary audit evidence. Infrastructure logs and protected backups may follow independent retention cycles. We have no automatic deletion or instant erasure promise for application receipts or backups; outcomes depend on identity verification and the law applicable to the person and records.

Depending on applicable law, applicants may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. Contact alpha7auto@gmail.com with the request type and associated account email. Do not send passwords, private keys, identity documents or payment details by email. ALPHA may require proportionate identity verification before making a change. An acknowledgment is not a completed deletion, approval or account closure.

Security and updates

Account restrictions use issuer-bound authentication, anti-forgery checks and tenant-limited access. No internet service is perfectly secure. Future material changes use an updated effective date and version; the browser and API must agree before accepting an application. Mandatory rights under applicable law are not waived by this notice.
