ACCOUNT REGISTRATION — LEGAL REVIEW DRAFT

Advertiser Account Privacy Notice — Draft

Not yet approved for live registration. This draft documents the registration implementation and is not an operative privacy notice until reviewed, approved, and published by ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC.

Proposed notice version: advertiser-privacy-2026-11-07-v1. The version identifier is reserved in the application; the effective date and final retention schedule require owner approval.

What this account flow collects

The sign-in step sends the email address and password directly over HTTPS to the configured Supabase Auth service. ALPHA's registration API does not receive the plaintext password. A separate step asks for a business name, two-letter country code, and optional HTTPS business website.

When an applicant confirms the required privacy-processing choice and terms acceptance, the registration service stores an opaque identity subject, the business profile, a unique request key, the two approved-version identifiers, consent and optional-marketing choices, a receipt identifier, a hash of the canonical request, and timestamps in its registration database. Email verification is required before the business registration endpoint accepts a pending applicant.

Purpose and access

This information is used for sign-in verification, preventing duplicate requests, recording the application and the choices made, security checks, and human review. A receipt means pending review; it does not grant a marketplace workspace or authorize paid advertising activity. Account roles are assigned separately only after authorized review. Optional marketing consent is unchecked and must not affect application eligibility.

Service providers and data sharing

Supabase provides the identity service, Railway hosts the application/database when authorized and deployed, and Vercel serves the preview/web interface. Relevant providers may process technical metadata. This account flow is distinct from the public advertiser-interest form and its CRM routing; submitting a business registration does not automatically authorize CRM marketing or ad purchases.

Retention, correction and deletion

The final retention periods for identity records, registration applications, immutable audit receipts and associated technical logs have **not** been approved. Before live enablement, ALPHA must publish a retention/erasure schedule consistent with legal obligations and the immutable-receipt design. Applicants can use the privacy request form to ask about access, correction, or deletion, subject to identity verification and lawful recordkeeping obligations.

Changes

Any approved change affecting the purposes or required choices must be assigned an updated notice version before new registrations are accepted.
