ADVERTISER ACCOUNT PRIVACY

Advertiser Account Privacy Notice

Effective November 20, 2026. Notice version advertiser-privacy-2026-11-20-v2. Controller: ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC (“ALPHA”). Account registration may remain disabled until its separate technical acceptance is complete. This notice describes the account application process when enabled, not an assertion that public signups are active.

Information we use and why

The identity provider, Supabase Auth, processes the email address, password, and email-verification/security details submitted directly to its HTTPS service. ALPHA's business registration API does not receive your plaintext password. After successful verified sign-in, the applicant may supply a business name, two-letter country code, and optional HTTPS website. The account application stores the opaque authenticated identity reference, business profile, request key and hash, required privacy/terms choices and version identifiers, separate optional-marketing choice, timestamps, and a pending-review registration receipt. Technical security logs may contain limited device/network information.

These records support identity verification, application review, duplicate/fraud prevention, authorization, security, and a record of what the applicant accepted. The application remains PENDING_REVIEW until a human decision. A signup or receipt is not a paid campaign order, creator booking, or automatic advertiser workspace approval.

Service providers and processing locations

Supabase provides identity services. Railway hosts the business API and its PostgreSQL database; Vercel may deliver public informational pages. These providers may process infrastructure or security data in regions different from your own country. Relevant transfers and retention depend on their actual infrastructure and legal obligations; we do not promise that all records remain in one country. Public advertiser interest forms and the optional HighLevel CRM follow-up system are separate from authenticated advertiser accounts. Account registration does not automatically opt you into CRM marketing.

Required choices and optional marketing

The application asks you to acknowledge this privacy notice and accept the advertiser account terms to submit an application. Optional marketing is a separate, initially unchecked choice and does not affect eligibility. No advertising message is authorized solely by creating an account. A prior immutable consent receipt records the choice at submission; it does not by itself prove continuing permission after a withdrawal.

Retention, deletion and backup limitations

We retain application information while needed to assess a pending application, administer an accepted account, protect security, respond to disputes and privacy requests, or meet a specific legal obligation. Following closure, withdrawal, or rejection, the controller reviews whether each category remains necessary and removes or restricts records when lawfully possible. Relevant audit receipts may need limited preservation to prove prior actions or comply with legal requirements. Operational security logs and independently held backups may follow separate provider retention cycles. We do not claim to have an automated account-deletion schedule or instant erasure of audit receipts and backups. Requests are assessed manually, including identity verification and legitimate retention exceptions.

Your requests and contact channel

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, object to certain processing, or withdraw optional marketing permission. Contact ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC's designated account privacy address, alpha7auto@gmail.com, describing the request type and the account email used. Do not email passwords, full identity documents, sensitive files, or payment credentials. We may require proportionate, secure identity verification before disclosing or changing an account. The technical account privacy-case form is a separate authenticated feature and may still be disabled; the public-interest privacy form is not an authenticated account-rights channel.

A case acknowledgment means only that the request was received for verification. It does not establish that erasure, optional-marketing suppression, or account closure has already happened. Any outcome is subject to applicable law and recorded human assessment; we do not promise a uniform international deadline or automated fulfillment.

Security and changes

We use issuer-bound authentication, role and tenant restrictions, anti-forgery safeguards and audit receipts in the account design. No system is guaranteed absolutely secure. Material changes to required processing or terms are versioned; the browser and server must present and record the same version for new submissions. This notice does not override mandatory rights under applicable law.
