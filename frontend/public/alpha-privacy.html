Privacy Notice — Public Interest Forms

Effective November 5, 2026 · Notice version AGM-INTEREST-2026-11-05

ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC offers public interest forms for creators and advertisers. This notice describes how information submitted through those forms is handled. The forms are separate from registered accounts, payment services and the protected Bliss Bot Chapel marketplace.

Information you choose to submit

We request your name, email address, and relevant creator channel or business information, market and optional message. We also record the choices you make about processing and optional marketing, an opaque request identifier, receipt identifier and the date and time the request was received. We ask you to confirm you are at least 18. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government IDs or other sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use your submitted details to record and review your interest, determine whether our services may fit and, if appropriate, respond about the request. Optional marketing updates are not necessary to request an evaluation and require a separate unchecked choice. Submitting does not create a commercial account or authorize a campaign.

Where information is stored

Public interest data is stored in a restricted database hosted on Supabase in its Singapore region for the Bliss Bot project. The webpage is hosted by Vercel. Submission data is sent over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function. The public database roles cannot directly read or write the intake records. Hosting services may process technical request metadata for security and delivery. These interest submissions are not automatically placed in the protected Railway marketplace database.

Retention

Intake requests and associated receipts are scheduled to be deleted after 90 days by a daily database cleanup job. Requests for earlier deletion can be made through the privacy request form. Platform security and operational logs may have separate provider-controlled retention.

Your choices and requests

You can decline to submit, and optional marketing is not preselected. To request access, correction or deletion of information in these public forms, use our privacy request form with the email you used when submitting. Requests are retained for review, and identity verification may be required before details are disclosed or changed. Processing times may depend on applicable law and necessary verification.

Changes

If our intake methods or privacy practices change, this page will be updated with an effective date and a new notice version for future submissions. This notice concerns only ALPHA's public interest forms, not the complete operations of other workspaces.
