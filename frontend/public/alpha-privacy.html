Privacy Notice — Public Interest Forms

Effective November 8, 2026 · Notice version AGM-INTEREST-2026-11-08-GHL-01

ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC offers public interest forms for creators and advertisers. This notice describes how information submitted through those forms is handled. The forms are separate from registered accounts, payment services and the protected Bliss Bot Chapel marketplace.

Information you choose to submit

We request your name, email address, and relevant creator channel or business information, market and optional message. We also record the choices you make about processing and optional marketing, an opaque request identifier, receipt identifier and the date and time the request was received. We ask you to confirm you are at least 18. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government IDs or other sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use your submitted details to record and review your interest, determine whether our services may fit and, if appropriate, respond about the request. Optional marketing updates are not necessary to request an evaluation and require a separate unchecked choice. Submitting does not create a commercial account or authorize a campaign.

Where information is stored and CRM sharing

Public interest data is stored in a restricted database hosted on Supabase in its Singapore region for the Bliss Bot project. The webpage is hosted by Vercel. Submission data is sent over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function. The public database roles cannot directly read or write the intake records. Hosting services may process technical request metadata for security and delivery. These interest submissions are not automatically placed in the protected Railway marketplace database. Eligible creator and advertiser inquiries that include the required processing acknowledgment are currently routed to ALPHA’s GoHighLevel (HighLevel/LeadConnector) CRM account for inquiry review and response. The contact sync sends the submitter’s name, email address, inquiry type, and, when provided, a creator’s public channel or website or an advertiser’s business name. It does not send optional free-text descriptions, privacy requests, marketing choices, or identity/payment records. This contact sync does not authorize marketing communications; optional marketing remains a separate choice. The CRM contact is held separately from the Supabase inquiry and receipt. The 90-day Supabase cleanup does not delete a HighLevel CRM contact. ALPHA has not verified the HighLevel account’s exact data-storage or processing locations, provider-controlled retention period, or remote deletion pathway. You can request access, correction, or deletion through the privacy request form; identity verification may be required, and submitting a request does not itself delete the HighLevel contact. This form integration does not itself launch automated email, SMS, or paid campaigns.

Retention

Intake requests and associated receipts in Supabase are scheduled to be deleted after 90 days by a daily database cleanup job. HighLevel CRM contacts are separate and are not automatically deleted by that Supabase cleanup. ALPHA has not verified a HighLevel automatic deletion schedule or remote deletion pathway. You may request access, correction, or deletion through the privacy request form; requests are reviewed and identity verification may be required. Submitting a request does not itself delete the CRM contact. Platform security and operational logs may have separate provider-controlled retention.

Your choices and requests

You can decline to submit, and optional marketing is not preselected. To request access, correction or deletion of information from these public forms or the linked GoHighLevel CRM, use our privacy request form with the email you used when submitting. Requests are retained for review, and identity verification may be required before details are disclosed or changed. Processing times may depend on applicable law and necessary verification.

Changes

If our intake methods or privacy practices change, this page will be updated with an effective date and a new notice version for future submissions. This notice concerns only ALPHA's public interest forms, not the complete operations of other workspaces or any future paid customer billing, service fulfillment or payment processing.
