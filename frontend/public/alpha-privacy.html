Privacy Notice — Public Interest Forms

Effective November 8, 2026 · Notice version AGM-FULL-SPECTRUM-BUDGET-2026-11-08-V1

ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC offers public interest forms for creators and advertisers. This notice describes how information submitted through those forms is handled. The forms are separate from registered accounts, payment services and the protected Bliss Bot Chapel marketplace.

Information you choose to submit

We request your name, email address, and relevant creator channel or advertiser business information. Advertiser inquiries also include an all-in budget and currency, target market, campaign objective, preferred media formats, audience requirements and preferred languages. We record your processing acknowledgment, age confirmation, separate contact consent, optional marketing choice, notice version, an opaque request identifier, receipt identifier and submission time. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government IDs or other sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use your submitted details to record and review your interest, determine whether our services may fit and, if appropriate, respond about the request. Optional marketing updates are not necessary to request an evaluation and require a separate unchecked choice. Submitting does not create a commercial account or authorize a campaign.

Where information is stored and CRM sharing

Public interest data is stored in a restricted database hosted on Supabase in its Singapore region for the Bliss Bot project. The webpage is hosted by Vercel. Submission data is sent over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function. The public database roles cannot directly read or write the intake records. Hosting services may process technical request metadata for security and delivery. These interest submissions are not automatically placed in the protected Railway marketplace database. Only creator and advertiser inquiries with separate contact consent are eligible for routing to ALPHA’s GoHighLevel (HighLevel/LeadConnector) CRM for inquiry review. Advertiser contact details and selected structured campaign preferences may be shared for that purpose. This contact consent does not authorize marketing communications; optional marketing remains a separate choice. CRM routing may be delayed or unavailable while required integration safeguards are not configured. The CRM contact is held separately from the Supabase inquiry and receipt. The 90-day Supabase cleanup does not delete a HighLevel CRM contact. ALPHA has not verified the HighLevel account’s exact data-storage or processing locations, provider-controlled retention period, or remote deletion pathway. You can request access, correction, or deletion through the privacy request form; identity verification may be required, and submitting a request does not itself delete the HighLevel contact. This form integration does not itself launch automated email, SMS, or paid campaigns.

Retention

Intake requests and associated receipts in Supabase are scheduled to be deleted after 90 days by a daily database cleanup job. HighLevel CRM contacts are separate and are not automatically deleted by that Supabase cleanup. ALPHA has not verified a HighLevel automatic deletion schedule or remote deletion pathway. You may request access, correction, or deletion through the privacy request form; requests are reviewed and identity verification may be required. Submitting a request does not itself delete the CRM contact. Platform security and operational logs may have separate provider-controlled retention.

Ask Alpha AI — optional conversation processing notice

Notice version AGM-ASK-ALPHA-AI-2026-11-08 · Issued November 8, 2026; owner authorized controlled activation on November 10, 2026. The optional AI service remains subject to secure project access, visitor identity protections and current-month billing checks. It may be unavailable while these controls are verified. Ask Alpha's optional AI mode is separate from the public interest forms above. Visitors can continue using scripted guided help without choosing AI processing. The optional AI-processing checkbox is not marketing consent.

What is sent: Only after an explicit AI-processing choice, Ask Alpha sends the current question and up to four recent conversation turns through ALPHA's existing Supabase Edge Function to the OpenAI Responses API to generate an informational answer. The current configuration uses GPT-6 Luna with response storage disabled (store:false), without provider tools or automated outreach. Visitors should not enter passwords, API keys, payment card data, telephone numbers, email addresses, identity records, or confidential business details; the chat attempts to reject contact and credential strings but cannot guarantee detection of all sensitive content.

What ALPHA stores: ALPHA's restricted database stores a limited technical receipt: AI-processing consent version and time, an anonymous request identifier, cryptographic hashes of the request/response, pseudonymous session and network-identity hashes, requested audience, model, OpenAI request reference, token counts, estimated cost and technical state. Ask Alpha does not intentionally store raw conversation text in its Supabase receipt table or automatically create HighLevel contacts, sales commitments, billing transactions, or human support cases from chat. The selected marketing checkbox is recorded separately and does not itself authorize CRM outreach.

What OpenAI may retain: OpenAI's API documentation states that API inputs and outputs are not used for training unless the account explicitly opts in. Although store:false disables the normal persistent Responses API application state, provider abuse-monitoring logs may contain submitted content and generally be retained for up to 30 days, with stated legal and safety exceptions. Hosting and security providers may also process technical request metadata. See OpenAI API data controls.

ALPHA receipt retention: A dedicated daily database cleanup job is designed to delete SETTLED pseudonymous Ask Alpha receipts once they are older than 90 days. Billing records in UNKNOWN or RESERVED state are retained beyond 90 days until an authorized operator reconciles their usage, so unresolved provider charges cannot be hidden or treated as free. This exception may delay deletion of a related technical receipt. The cleanup job and its actual deletion behavior must be independently verified in the intended hosted environment before public AI activation. OpenAI and other infrastructure providers have separate retention practices.

Accuracy and human help: AI responses can be inaccurate and are not approved advertising prices, legal terms, refund decisions or binding commitments. Chat is not a support case. Use the human-contact page or privacy request form for a staffed reply or data-rights request. ALPHA may need to verify identity before disclosing or deleting any personal information.

Your choices and requests

You can decline to submit, and optional marketing is not preselected. To request access, correction or deletion of information from these public forms or the linked GoHighLevel CRM, use our privacy request form with the email you used when submitting. Requests are retained for review, and identity verification may be required before details are disclosed or changed. Processing times may depend on applicable law and necessary verification.

Changes

If our intake methods or privacy practices change, this page will be updated with an effective date and a new notice version for future submissions. This notice concerns only ALPHA's public interest forms, not the complete operations of other workspaces or any future paid customer billing, service fulfillment or payment processing.
