Privacy Notice — Public Interest and Support Forms

Interest forms: November 5, 2026 (AGM-INTEREST-2026-11-05-GHL). Proposed support form: November 8, 2026 (AGM-SUPPORT-2026-11-08); support intake is not live until separately approved and deployed

ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC offers public interest forms for creators and advertisers and a proposed separate support-case intake. This notice describes how information submitted through those forms is handled. The forms are separate from registered accounts, payment services and the protected Bliss Bot Chapel marketplace.

Information you choose to submit

We request your name, email address, and relevant creator channel or business information, market and optional message. We also record the choices you make about processing and optional marketing, an opaque request identifier, receipt identifier and the date and time the request was received. We ask you to confirm you are at least 18. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, government IDs or other sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use your submitted details to record and review your interest, determine whether our services may fit and, if appropriate, respond about the request. Optional marketing updates are not necessary to request an evaluation and require a separate unchecked choice. Submitting does not create a commercial account or authorize a campaign.

Where information is stored and CRM sharing

Public interest data is stored in a restricted database hosted on Supabase in its Singapore region for the Bliss Bot project. The webpage is hosted by Vercel. Submission data is sent over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function. The public database roles cannot directly read or write the intake records. Hosting services may process technical request metadata for security and delivery. These interest submissions are not automatically placed in the protected Railway marketplace database. If the owner activates the GoHighLevel (HighLevel/LeadConnector) CRM integration, limited creator and advertiser contact data (name, email, business name or public channel/website, and inquiry type) is shared with HighLevel under the selected sub-account for lead review and response. Free-text messages, privacy-rights inquiries and identity/payment records are not forwarded. HighLevel may process and store data in locations outside Singapore and the Philippines under its service and privacy practices. Sending an inquiry does not authorize marketing messages; those require separate optional consent. This form integration does not itself launch automated email, SMS, or paid campaigns.

Support and complaints (proposed, not live)

The proposed support form records name, reply email, category and brief description in the same restricted Supabase intake, plus a case ID, category-based severity, routing role, status, assigned operator ID if later assigned, and resolution history. General, advertiser and creator support name and email may be sent to HighLevel only after the separate support CRM switch and owner review are enabled. Billing, refunds, failed campaigns, privacy and security cases are excluded from general CRM delivery. Case details, security reports, free-text descriptions and resolution notes are never sent by the general CRM adapter. Human operators must be designated before a response or escalation channel can be claimed to be staffed. Do not submit secrets, payment-card data, IDs or detailed vulnerabilities.

Retention

Intake requests and associated receipts in Supabase are scheduled to be deleted after 90 days by a daily database cleanup job. If the support workflow is later deployed, its associated case and event history is also designed to be deleted with the parent intake request under that retention process, subject to legal preservation obligations. CRM contact records synced to HighLevel, if enabled, are separate and are not automatically deleted by that Supabase cleanup; they may be retained for inquiry management until deleted through an authorized review or privacy request. Requests for earlier deletion can be made through the privacy request form. Platform security and operational logs may have separate provider-controlled retention.

Your choices and requests

You can decline to submit, and optional marketing is not preselected. To request access, correction or deletion of information from these public forms or the linked GoHighLevel CRM, use our privacy request form with the email you used when submitting. Requests are retained for review, and identity verification may be required before details are disclosed or changed. Processing times may depend on applicable law and necessary verification.

Changes

If our intake methods or privacy practices change, this page will be updated with an effective date and a new notice version for future submissions. This notice concerns only ALPHA's public interest forms, not the complete operations of other workspaces or any future paid customer billing, service fulfillment or payment processing.
