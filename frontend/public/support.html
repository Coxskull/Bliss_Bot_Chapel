Questions, support and disputes

For assistance with an ALPHA inquiry or proposed service, contact the owner-designated business mailbox: dejesuserwin1@gmail.com. This address currently handles manual business enquiries. A staffed support desk, ticket system and guaranteed response time are not yet available.

No automatic case number is issued by email. Keep your sent message and any reference receipt from an earlier inquiry. We may need to verify your identity and relevant order before sharing account or financial information. Sending an email does not approve a refund, change an order, create a paid support contract or guarantee an outcome.

Choose the appropriate route

  • General enquiries or previous inquiry receipts: send a general support email. Include an existing inquiry receipt if you have one, but no confidential records.
  • Advertiser questions: submit a new advertiser enquiry or email about an existing enquiry.
  • Creator questions: submit a new creator enquiry or email about an existing enquiry.
  • Billing, cancellations, refunds or failed delivery: send a service concern. Do not email payment card details. Any applicable remedy depends on the actual written order, applicable law and verified transaction; no automatic credit or refund is offered here.
  • Privacy access, correction or deletion: use the privacy request form to receive an existing durable enquiry receipt. Do not include government IDs or passwords in the form.
  • Suspected security issues: send a short security concern without passwords, tokens, exploit details or sensitive customer records. This mailbox is not an emergency incident-response service; seek appropriate local emergency or incident assistance for immediate harm.

What happens next?

Messages require manual review and may not be answered immediately. The owner-designated mailbox is not evidence of 24-hour staffing or a completed case-management system. ALPHA does not use this contact page to collect payments, operate live campaigns, or issue refunds.
