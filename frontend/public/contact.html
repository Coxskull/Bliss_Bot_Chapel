ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC · CONTACT

Contact ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC

ALPHA currently accepts public inquiries through the following forms. They are recorded as public inquiries, not purchases, authenticated customer accounts or paid support tickets. A successful submission displays a reference receipt; retain it for follow-up. Response timing is not guaranteed.

Advertising and business inquiries

For questions about advertising planning, creator collaboration, proposals or potential service scope, use the advertiser inquiry form. Include the nature of your request in the message field; submitting provides an on-screen reference receipt when successfully recorded.

Creator and publisher inquiries

Creators seeking information about collaboration may use the creator inquiry form to introduce their public channel and ask a question.

Privacy and personal information requests

For access, correction or deletion of information provided through public forms, use the privacy request form. Do not put passwords, government identification or card numbers in any public form.

Customer support and case tracking

Use the support case form for general assistance, advertiser or creator help, billing or refund questions, failed-campaign complaints, privacy concerns, or security reports. A recorded case and receipt can be quoted for follow-up; a human response is not guaranteed until staffing is confirmed. Do not share passwords, account tokens, identification numbers or payment-card data.

Online paid orders are not currently available. A verified staffed support contact and service-specific dispute/cancellation instructions must still be supplied in the written order before any paid service starts.

To understand proposed services before contacting us, see Advertising services and proposals. Please do not submit a payment through an unverified third-party link.
