Advertiser Account Registration Terms — Draft

Not yet approved for live registration. These terms must be reviewed and approved by ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC before accepting real account applications.

Proposed terms version: advertiser-terms-2026-11-07-v1. This version identifier is reserved by the application; it is not a currently effective contract.

Registration versus an inquiry

This flow requests a verified sign-in and a separate business-registration application. It is distinct from the public advertiser-interest form. Business registration creates a pending-review record and an audit receipt, not an automatically approved marketplace workspace.

Eligibility and accuracy

The applicant must be legally authorized to submit business details and use the email address provided. The application requires email verification, a business name, two-letter country code and accurate information. Do not submit another person's confidential information, payment credentials, government identifiers or prohibited content.

No automatic approval or charge

ALPHA may review an application before granting any marketplace access. An acceptance receipt or sign-in confirmation alone does not grant permissions, place an ad, promise creator availability, establish paid advertising terms or create a payment obligation. Paid services, if separately offered, require a distinct approved proposal, payment terms, and fulfillment agreement.

Consent and account security

The applicant must explicitly accept the approved registration privacy notice and these terms. Optional marketing permission is separate, initially unchecked, and is not required to apply. Applicants should safeguard their credentials and report suspected unauthorized access via Contact.

Data rights and changes

The proposed use of account information is described in the draft advertiser account privacy notice. Before live activation, ALPHA must approve final retention, dispute/contact, and account-review processes, and publish a final effective date. Revised versions require the applicant to accept the versions current at submission.
