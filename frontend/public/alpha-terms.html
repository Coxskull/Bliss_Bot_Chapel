Scope: These terms govern only public interest inquiries. They are not commercial advertising service terms. Paid services, if offered in the future, require separately approved service descriptions, cancellation/refund rules, customer support and a written customer order before any payment.

These forms allow adults to express interest in ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC's creator–advertiser marketplace. A submission is an inquiry only. It is not an approved membership application, verified identity, agreement to advertise, guarantee of creator earnings or proposal for a paid campaign.

Provide accurate information about yourself, your channel or your business. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share or content that is unlawful, misleading or infringes others' rights. Do not submit financial credentials, account passwords, or sensitive government-issued identification.

ALPHA may review inquiries and decide whether to contact the sender. We make no promise of response, matched advertisers, campaign placements, monetization, or commercial outcomes. We handle information supplied through these forms as stated in the Privacy Notice. With the updated notice, limited creator and advertiser contact details may also be stored in GoHighLevel CRM if enabled. CRM retention is separate from the 90-day Supabase form purge. Any marketing communication requires a separate optional selection; the processing acknowledgement is solely for reviewing the inquiry.

Contact and requests

For requests about personal information submitted through the forms, use our privacy request form. For a new inquiry, use the relevant creator or advertiser form. To find current public contact routes, see Contact ALPHA.
