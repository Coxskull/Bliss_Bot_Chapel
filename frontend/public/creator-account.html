How account setup works

The public interest form and the protected creator account are separate steps. An interest receipt is not a login, verification badge, or approval.

  1. Submit creator interest once.Share your public channel, market and content format using the existing form. Keep the receipt you receive.
  2. Wait for manual verification.ALPHA reviews the channel and confirms the creator record. Submitting a form does not automatically create an account.
  3. Receive an invitation from ALPHA.Only an approved creator receives an individual secure account invitation. Never share or forward its link.
  4. Set your password from the invitation email.Open the original secure invitation link to reach the password setup form. The setup form will reject a missing or expired invitation.
