CREATOR ACCOUNT REGISTRATION

How registration works

The public interest form and account registration are separate. A registration receipt confirms submission only; manual review is required before creator verification or private dashboard access.

  1. Submit creator interest once.Share your public channel, market and content format using the existing form. Keep the receipt you receive.
  2. Create a verified sign-in.Your password goes directly to Supabase Auth. ALPHA's application does not receive or store it.
  3. Submit creator details and consent.Privacy and terms consent are required; optional marketing consent is recorded separately.
  4. Wait for manual review.New creator records remain unverified and pending review. Registration alone grants no marketplace or dashboard authority.
