# ALPHA-346 — Fictional four-deliverable planning demonstration
**FICTIONAL ONLY / NOT CUSTOMER WORK / NOT A SALE / NOT A LIVE CAMPAIGN**

This exercise shows what the one-campaign service can produce entirely by human planning and reusable templates, without marketplace databases, third-party rights, media spend, creator outreach or a paid API.

## Fictional brief — Riverfern Café (invented example)
- Product: a hypothetical local café offering a made-to-order iced citrus drink.
- Market: a hypothetical city-center neighborhood; English sample copy (no real address or customer).
- Objective: develop a **planning direction** for increasing awareness of the fictional drink, not a revenue or reach guarantee.
- Audience hypothesis: local adult café visitors who enjoy short beverage demonstrations. **Unverified hypothetical audience insight.**
- Proposed message: "Your afternoon, freshly mixed." **Fictional copy requiring real claim/brand review before reuse.**
- Proposed CTA: "Explore the menu." **No live URL, sale or traffic tracking.**
- Claim substantiation: NONE. Avoid claims about health, origin, sustainability or numerical performance.
- Brand assets: FICTIONAL PLACEHOLDERS ONLY. No actual brand/logo licensed.
- Approval status: SYNTHETIC TEST; NOT CUSTOMER APPROVED.

## Fictional format suitability matrix
| Format | Reasoned suitability | Benefits | Dependencies / caveats | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short-form beverage demonstration | Illustrates preparation clearly in a short story | Visual product clarity; a simple CTA | Requires separate paid production, talent/footage rights, platform review | Preferred **concept for further planning** |
| Livestream sponsor overlay | Could announce a drink alongside a suitable live show | Reusable branded graphic in a permitted zone | Requires a willing creator, independently accepted geometry and disclosure | Secondary concept, NO INVENTORY |
| Podcast audio mention | Can describe the product and a story | Good for conversational storytelling | No visuals; requires verified audio rights and willing creator | Defer pending audience evidence |

These are not creator recommendations, bookings, audience-size estimates or media-buy commitments.

## Fictional creative direction sheet
- Hero idea: an original, unbranded illustrated cold drink with citrus slices in soft warm afternoon light; not a reproduction of another brand's campaign.
- Hierarchy: hypothetical product visual → short headline → optional menu CTA → any required sponsored-content disclosure.
- Mood: clean, fresh and welcoming; avoid medical/therapeutic/unsupported sustainability claims.
- Potential production formats: vertical short-form storyboard or landscape companion graphic, only if **separately commissioned**.
- Art requirements: permissioned customer logo/photos; contrast/legibility and caption review; actual image/video licensing and creator-safe areas checked separately.
- Deliverable now: direction and requirements only; **NO FINISHED AD**, NO IMAGE CREATED, NO CREATOR PARTICIPATION.
