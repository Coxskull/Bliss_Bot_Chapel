## Included deliverables — exactly one of each, except revisions
1. **Campaign brief:** written objective, one product/service, desired audience, market, campaign message, intended CTA, customer-provided claims and restrictions; record assumptions.
2. **Channel and creator-format suitability matrix:** up to three *categories/formats* (for example podcasts, livestreams or short-form creator videos), with assumptions, compatibility criteria and tradeoffs. This is **not** a list of verified available creators.
3. **Creative direction sheet:** messaging hierarchy, art/asset checklist, format/placement considerations, brand-review checkpoints, ad-disclosure requirements and customer approvals needed. **No finished video or placed ad is included.**
4. **Execution coordination plan:** proposed tasks, owner/advertiser responsibilities, dependency/risk register, review milestones and handoff checklist. Any real creator outreach, reservation or launch is a **separate signed engagement**.
5. **One consolidated revision round:** one customer-provided set of revisions to the four documents above, within the agreed scope. A substantive scope change requires a new quote and schedule.

Delivery format: electronic PDF or agreed editable document, plus a brief completion note. A human coordinator checks source facts, brand permissions and delivery before providing the final files.
