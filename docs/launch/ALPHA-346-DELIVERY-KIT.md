# ALPHA-346 — First Customer Manual Delivery Kit
**Status:** READY FOR INTERNAL HUMAN USE; NOT A CUSTOMER CONTRACT OR APPROVAL TO CHARGE
**Service:** One-Campaign Planning & Coordination
**Scope:** Exactly four planning deliverables + one in-scope consolidated revision.

## One operator workflow
1. Review a customer-initiated advertiser inquiry in the existing approved private CRM/operator path; correlate the saved receipt; do not copy names/emails into a public repo.
2. Review actual customer goals and business scope against `ALPHA-346-ONE-CAMPAIGN-PLANNING-SERVICE.md`. Confirm that actual outreach, purchased media, creator booking and finished ads are excluded.
3. Obtain Captain/Finance/Legal approvals and customer acceptance in the completed private Order. Use `ALPHA-346-FIRST-CUSTOMER-CAPTAIN-DECISIONS.md` to identify missing support, pricing, policies and payment release. Inquiry alone never authorizes work.
4. Copy the four templates listed below into a **restricted** customer workspace; replace every placeholder with validated customer information. Do not store customer personal data in GitHub.
5. Produce planning drafts, clearly label assumptions/unknowns, and ask for **one consolidated round of feedback**. Record revisions and customer approval without implying creator commitments.
6. Perform human QA and deliver final electronic documents through the authorized customer-response channel; retain handoff and acceptance proof privately. Close or escalate per signed Order and approved policy.
7. Keep money handling in a separately authorized secure processor and reconciliation process. Never obtain payment-card details through public interest forms or email.

## Hard separation of statuses
- **INQUIRY_RECEIVED**: server receipt saved, not a verified customer, no payment or marketing consent implied.
- **PROPOSAL_READY**: complete priced Order and approved versions prepared and approved internally; no send or charge implied.
- **CUSTOMER_APPROVED**: specific human acceptance of price, exact scope and terms, not proof of payment.
- **PAYMENT_CONFIRMED**: verified secure processor funds status under separate payment-release authority, not inferred from a redirect or CRM.
- **DELIVERED**: final four planning documents handed over and customer receipt noted; **not** an ad placement.
- **CLOSED**: customer acceptance and financial/support reconciliation documented in protected records.
