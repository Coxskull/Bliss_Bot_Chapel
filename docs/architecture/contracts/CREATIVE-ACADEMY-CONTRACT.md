## Objective

Produce original advertisements that compete with or exceed an approved reference's perceived commercial production quality. Teach production work from an approved visual prototype and a written recipe. Creative DNA customizes the advertiser. The instructor judges the result. Only approved work moves forward.

The purpose of the Academy is production. This local proof prepares the production specification, anchor selection, casting direction, and QA gates. It does not pretend that an advertisement was generated when no production model is configured.

The objective is not for 100 advertisements to look alike. The objective is for 100 advertisers to look like 100 professionally designed brands at one quality floor.

**Governing doctrine:** reproduce the craftsmanship; replace the creative content. Originality without quality parity is failure. Quality parity without originality is failure.

## The method

The prototype is the finished lesson. It teaches the quality floor.

The production specification is the recipe. It explains why the prototype works: photorealism, lighting, contrast, depth, typography, composition, product realism, visual hierarchy, the call to action, brand identity, and creator protection.

Creative DNA decides who the advertiser is. A restaurant, a resort, a dental clinic, a motorcycle dealer, a supermarket, a coffee company, and a fitness center are not siblings.

The production worker creates the advertisement. The Creative Instructor compares it with the quality floor and returns PASS, REVISE, or FAIL. A critical defect overrides a numerical score. A revision names what to preserve and what to repair. The rest of a strong advertisement is not thrown away.

## Required flow

1. A prospect is discovered.
2. Business and market research is stored.
3. Creative DNA is created.
4. The appropriate prototype family is selected.
5. The reference image and the production specification are read together.
6. The reference quality signature is extracted: tone, color, lighting, depth, material realism, hero dominance, hierarchy, and commercial polish.
7. Advertiser Brand DNA, customization, inventory requirements, and geographic context are read.
8. A new creative direction replaces the brand, photography, product, people, environment, copy, and composition.
9. A candidate advertisement is produced.
10. The Creative Instructor scores it and names defects.
11. Brand/customization compliance, quality parity, originality, and geographic authenticity are judged independently.
12. A fully passed candidate becomes eligible to continue through the approval workflow. REVISE returns a preserve list and a repair list, then the candidate is inspected again. FAIL stops automatic approval.

This run stores the supplied curriculum, prepares a deterministic production brief, and judges the independent gates. It does not call a model or claim that a new advertisement was generated.

## Creative DNA

Creative DNA can record the business category, country, city, brand personality, target customer, imagery, exact colors, font family, typography character, hero product, headline, supporting copy, call to action, products, offers, language, cultural context, brand voice, commercial tone, dimensions, placement, QR destination, inventory format, restrictions, and creator-safe areas.

The advertiser's approved Brand DNA is authoritative for its identity. The quality anchor is not the advertiser's brand manual. A request for burgundy and gold must use burgundy and gold while preserving contrast, richness, and depth. A request for Montserrat must use Montserrat while preserving hierarchy and legibility.

This proof records family, brand, hero, palette, call to action, personality, and whether a teacher is on file. Copies of the Maison Fleur and VidaCare identities are refused.

## Creative Instructor

The instructor does not ask whether a picture is nice. It compares the candidate with the quality floor.

Configured weights, initial total 100:

| Category | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Typography and text accuracy | 16 |
| Lighting and depth | 12 |
| Product and subject realism | 12 |
| Composition and placement | 12 |
| Visual hierarchy | 10 |
| Brand identity | 10 |
| Commercial credibility | 10 |
| Creator protection | 10 |
| Call to action | 8 |

Weights must total 100. They are not permanently hard-coded.

A visual category stays unrecorded until an operator records the visual benchmark. This run does not invent a visual score from pixels.

The full reference parity gate records PASS or FAIL for:

1. Color power
2. Tonal contrast
3. Lighting craftsmanship
4. Highlight brilliance
5. Shadow quality
6. Dimensional depth
7. Foreground/background separation
8. Material realism
9. Texture fidelity
10. Hero dominance
11. Visual hierarchy
12. Typographic quality
13. Screen pop
14. Commercial polish
15. Premium Dominant Presence
16. Overall reference quality parity

An attractive, original, professional advertisement still fails when it cannot compete with the reference in a blind side-by-side quality-class comparison.

Critical defects block automatic approval:

- Misspelled or malformed important text
- A duplicated word
- A copied prototype identity
- An obstructed creator face

An obstructed creator face and a copied prototype identity are FAIL. A duplicated word or malformed text is REVISE. Clean text with no recorded visual benchmark is WITHHELD.

## Geographic authenticity

Geographic context is an input to Creative DNA, not a stereotype engine.

Initial markets include the Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia, Colombia, Panama, and the Dominican Republic. “Asian” and “Latino” are not sufficient casting instructions. Alpha uses a specific market, contemporary local commercial context, the campaign audience, the advertiser's approved assets, and varied original people.

Localization is not reduced to skin color. It considers facial plausibility, a realistic range of skin tones, hair, age, clothing, grooming, professional role, environment, and campaign context together. It does not caricature features, infer personality or economic status from ethnicity, or force traditional clothing without a campaign reason.

When confidence is insufficient, status is `MARKET_RESEARCH_REQUIRED`. Multiple legitimate references inform original casting. When approved employees, spokespersons, models, or photography are supplied, those advertiser assets take precedence.

Local authenticity does not lower requirements for skin, hair, eyes, anatomy, hands, clothing materials, lighting integration, depth, and premium commercial photography.
