Purpose

The Academy exists to produce original advertisements at or above an approved reference's perceived commercial production quality. This local phase records what the production worker and instructor must preserve. It does not claim that a production model generated an advertisement.

Governing doctrine

REPRODUCE THE CRAFTSMANSHIP.
REPLACE THE CREATIVE CONTENT.

Originality without quality parity is failure.
Quality parity without originality is failure.

Deep tonal contrast, vibrant controlled color, rich saturation, brilliant controlled highlights, deep readable shadows, directional hero lighting, edge and rim separation, specular detail, foreground/background separation, dimensional depth, photorealistic rendering, material realism, texture fidelity, professional depth of field, elegant bokeh, hero dominance, screen pop, typography, commercial polish, and Premium Dominant Presence.
