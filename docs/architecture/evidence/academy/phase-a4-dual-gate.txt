Purpose

Customization and quality are separate requirements. Neither replaces the other. Originality and geographic authenticity are also explicit gates.

Gate 1: Brand and customization compliance

Did the work respect approved branding, colors, fonts, copy, products, voice, imagery, requirements, restrictions, and requested customization?

Gate 2: Reference quality parity

Does the work compete with or exceed the master in lighting, realism, contrast, depth, texture, hierarchy, screen impact, commercial polish, and Premium Dominant Presence?

Gate 3: Originality

Did the work replace the reference's photograph, product, people, environment, brand, copy, supporting imagery, and creative expression?

Gate 4: Geographic authenticity

When people are shown, are they plausible for the specific market without caricature, generic regional casting, or one permanent national face?
