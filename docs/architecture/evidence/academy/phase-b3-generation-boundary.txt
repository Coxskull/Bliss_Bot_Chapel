Critical distinction

PRODUCTION_SPEC_READY means the niche anchor, client identity, Brand DNA, originality rules, quality signature, geographic direction, format, and QA requirements have been assembled into a production recipe.

It does not mean an advertisement image exists.

Why the refusal is required

The system must not:

claim that a recipe is an ad,

invent credentials,
purchase a model,
or mark a nonexistent image approved.

Future completion condition

An authorized production image model must accept the reference image plus the production recipe, return an original candidate, preserve important text correctly, and send the candidate through customization, quality parity, originality, geographic authenticity, critical-defect, and advertiser-review gates.
