Purpose

Creative DNA decides who the advertiser is. The prototype decides the quality floor. Two advertisers in the same niche must not become recolored copies of each other.

Decisions proved

Atelier Cendre is a distinct patisserie. Hero: burnt honey mille-feuille. Palette: ink and apricot. Call to action: Reserve your table. Personality: quiet coastal luxury. The teacher on file is maison-fleur. The brand stays distinct.

North Clinic is a dental practice. No prototype is on file for dental. None was invented. The teacher key is empty.

A request to store the brand Maison Fleur is refused. The prototype identity is the teacher. A new advertiser is not cloned.

Known families with no teacher on file

Restaurant, hotel, automotive, motorcycle, fitness, dental, medical, beauty, real estate, grocery, coffee, education, and financial services.

What this phase does not claim

Selecting a teacher does not approve an advertisement. It does not send a message. It does not open Wedding Planner. It does not invent a price.

Done when

The three DNA outcomes are visible: a distinct patisserie, a refused clone, and a family with no teacher.
