# Economics & Rate Intelligence — Phase Roadmap

Each phase requires its own contract, implementation, automated tests,
evidence, snapshots, screen recording, review, and acceptance. Do not
auto-advance.

| Phase | Scope | Primary output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reference-data foundation | Markets, pricing-model catalog, research sources, benchmark observations, read-only operator visibility |
| 2 | Versioned creator/audience economics | Audience and performance snapshots with provenance |
| 3 | Versioned market and industry economics | Economic profiles, inventory benchmarks, exchange-rate observations |
| 4 | Deterministic recommendation engine | Low/target/high ranges, confidence, factors, sources, pricing-rule version |
| 5 | Quotes and negotiation history | Quote/version/line-item/outcome records; human approvals |
| 6 | Compensation policy separation | Versioned participant rules and illustrations; no universal 20/80 |
| 7 | Public research orchestration | n8n jobs + AI extraction staging; .NET validation and durable provenance |
| 8 | Wedding Planner handshake | Planner asks Economics; matching and Planner never invent prices |
| 9 | Historical learning | Placement actuals, campaign performance, external-vs-Alpha comparisons |

Phase numbering belongs to `Bliss.Economics`; it does not renumber or
interrupt Bliss matching Phases 1–19 or Wedding Planner Phases 1–9.
