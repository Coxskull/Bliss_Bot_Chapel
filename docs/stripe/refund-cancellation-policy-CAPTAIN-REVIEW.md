### 1. Application and precedence (PROPOSED)
This policy would apply only to separately approved, individually quoted services sold by **ALPHA GLOBAL MEDIA LLC** (the "Provider") to an identified advertiser or business customer (the "Customer"). A public creator/advertiser interest form is not an order, reservation, account, charge, invoice or campaign commitment. The accepted written quote or statement of work ("Order") must identify the merchant of record, agreed scope, milestone schedule, currencies, deliverables, any approved creator/third-party commitments, cancellation terms, and policy version. If an Order proposes a variation, show it to the Customer *before* commitment and obtain express written agreement. Nothing here limits nonwaivable consumer, payment-card, privacy or other legal rights; counsel must verify governing jurisdictions and conflicts.

### 2. No charge or obligation from an inquiry
No fee, deposit, reservation, campaign execution, placement, creator engagement or payment is triggered by submitting an interest form. A potential Order becomes binding only after provider and customer accept the final proposal and payment activation is separately authorized. The website currently has **no approved live payment checkout**.

### 3. Before services start (PROPOSED ALLOCATION — REQUIRES DECISION)
Proposed customer-favorable default: if the Customer cancels in writing **before work begins** and **before any expressly authorized and disclosed nonrecoverable third-party commitments**, refund any payment collected under that Order in full. If the Customer authorized and was informed of a nonrecoverable third-party amount in the accepted Order, how it affects the refund must be identified there, subject to law and proof of that commitment. Do not impose undisclosed charges or assume a creator was contracted.

**Owner decision:** approve full-refund default? Define whether pre-start approved outside costs may be deducted; what proof and pre-authorization are required. Until approved, this is NOT a promise.

### 4. After work begins (PROPOSED ALLOCATION — REQUIRES DECISION)
On a written cancellation request, stop **new uncommitted work** after the request is acknowledged, while preserving evidence and avoiding unauthorized cancellation of third-party contracts. The Provider would give an itemized work-to-date statement and a breakdown of completed/accepted deliverables, reasonably documented work in progress, separately authorized nonrecoverable third-party commitments, taxes/fees, and any unperformed scope. The **proposed** refund basis is amounts attributable to *unperformed or undelivered services*, less only properly disclosed, lawful and documented incurred costs; disputed portions are reviewed before final determination. No automatic forfeiture, percentage, fee or nonrefundable deposit is approved by this draft.

**Owner decision:** define milestone allocation, treatment of work in progress, treatment of digital deliverables already provided, any lawful administrative fees, and what happens when a creator cancels.

### 5. Revisions and deliverable disagreement
The accepted Order must list the review/revision cycle, what constitutes an accepted deliverable, and who can approve material creative changes. If the Customer reports nonconformity, Provider should document the discrepancy and first offer a reasonable, order-consistent correction when feasible. If correction is not possible or is materially unsuccessful, assess an appropriate partial/full refund or other lawful remedy based on the missed contractual scope. A change of mind after accepting conforming work is different from nonperformance, but neither eliminates mandatory rights.

**Owner decision:** approve revision count, deadlines, acceptance method and escalation rights per order.

### 6. Nonperformance, unavailable creators, postponement or failure
No public inquiry guarantees any creator, impressions, results or placement. If the Provider cannot supply an accepted deliverable or the verified creator withdraws, promptly disclose the issue, offer a documented reasonable reschedule or substitute **only with the Customer's consent**, or apply an agreed, lawful refund for the undelivered work. Provider-caused nonperformance must not be disguised as a customer's cancellation or shift undisclosed risk to the Customer. No substitute creator/format is automatic.

Customer contacts the **owner-approved, monitored business support channel: [SUPPORT EMAIL / TELEPHONE OR VERIFIED SUPPORT FORM — CAPTAIN TO APPROVE]** and references the Order/receipt ID (avoid submitting full card data or sensitive credentials). Provider should acknowledge the request, preserve the original scope, milestone and delivery evidence, review the issue objectively, and communicate a reasoned decision and any proposed remedy through a verified channel.

**Proposed service standards for approval:** acknowledgement within **[N] business days**; initial decision within **[M] business days** unless verification/investigation requires more time, with a status update; escalation point **[RESPONSIBLE CONTACT / ROLE]**. None are effective until completed and approved. Define time zones, business days, and the official channel.

### 8. Refund execution and timing
Only authorized finance personnel may approve or initiate a refund after validating the original transaction, payment recipient and applicable legal/contractual entitlement. If a refund is approved, return it via the **original payment method where supported and permitted**, using the payment processor's secure workflow; do not request card numbers in email/forms. The Provider should confirm the initiated refund amount, currency and reference, and explain that the time to appear depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

**Owner/Finance decision:** approval levels, processing target **[N] business days after final approval**, who absorbs fees/taxes, currency conversion risk and how refund liability is reconciled with any creator compensation. Never guarantee an exact card-credit deadline unless verified with the processor.

### 9. Payment disputes, chargebacks and statutory rights
Customers may contact Provider first for a resolution but are **not required to waive or delay lawful chargeback/dispute rights**. The Provider should cooperate with card networks and the payment processor, respond through the authorized dashboard within required deadlines, and retain only relevant lawful evidence: accepted quote, notices, service milestones, content approval, creator delivery, correspondence and refund records. Do not contact or penalize a customer for exercising legitimate dispute rights. Avoid duplicate refunds while a payment dispute is active; follow the processor's guidance and counsel's advice.

### 10. Eligibility, complaint privacy and recordkeeping
Any signed quote must identify business entity, official support route, service limits, jurisdiction and order ID. Customer complaints should be handled by authorized personnel, access-controlled, and retained under the approved commercial Privacy Notice and legal retention schedule. Personal data and invoices must never be disclosed to unrelated advertisers, creators or public CRM links.

### 11. Changes and version control
Do not apply later policy changes retroactively without lawful agreement. Show the effective version and link it before collection of any payment. Every paid Order must record the version or attach it. Publish only after the Captain/Finance/Counsel record: approved text, policy version, date, jurisdiction and support route.
