## Owner approvals required before representing the site as ready to process payments
1. **Business identity:** confirm legal entity, Stripe registered country, public business address and name as submitted to Stripe. No personal/residential address may be inferred or published without owner approval.
2. **Customer support:** approve at least one REAL, MONITORED business support email or support phone number (ideally domain-verified), expected support availability and response handling for paid orders. Existing inquiry forms are not a confirmed post-purchase helpdesk.
3. **Actual product/quote/currency:** approve available ad-planning/creative/creator services, merchant of record, deliverables, standard scope, supported transaction currency, fees and client acceptance steps. No fixed-price offer is required if fully custom quoted, but each accepted order must disclose the price and currency before paying.
4. **Delivery:** approve fulfillment method, expected turnaround or timeline per scoped order, proof/acceptance, revisions, and what happens when the creator or service is unavailable.
5. **Refund/cancel/dispute:** approve real policy and URL describing which services are refundable/cancellable, when cancellation takes effect, how a customer requests it, and how refunds/disputes are processed; include service/production stage exceptions and applicable-law exceptions. Do not invent deadlines, fees, nonrefundable amounts or guarantees. Have legal/commercial owner approve actual terms before publishing.
6. **Commercial privacy/terms:** publish privacy practices for paid client records, processor sharing, invoices, retention and customer rights; publish paid service terms independent of currently published public-interest form terms.
7. **Checkout:** complete Stripe merchant approval and payment integration acceptance before showing Stripe as an accepted payment option, taking a deposit or promising immediate booking. Do not handle card details in forms.
8. **Testing:** browser-test public contact, privacy, paid policy, mobile navigation, every important CTA; separately test synthetic inquiry receipt only in an explicitly isolated environment or with owner-approved non-marketing test data. Verify owned domain controls and no login wall for public policy pages.
